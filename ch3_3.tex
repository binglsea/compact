	\section{Expandable spaces} \label{sec:3_3}


{\definition{}
\textup{(i)} $X$ is 
\uwave{$\lambda$-expandable}\index{$\lambda$-expandable| \S\ref{sec:ch3_3}\hfill} 
\textup{(Krajewski [1971]\textsuperscript{\cite{Krajewski}})} $($resp. 
\uwave{$\lambda$-discretely expandable}
\index{$\lambda$-discretely expandable| \S\ref{sec:ch3_3}\hfill}$)$ 
if every locally finite  $($resp. discrete$)$ $\lambda$-family 
of $($closed$)$ subsets of $X$ has a locally finite open expansion.

\textup{(ii)}  $X$ is 
\uwave{$\lambda$-almost expandable}\index{$\lambda$-almost expandable| \S\ref{sec:ch3_3}\hfill} 
\textup{(Smith and Krajewski [1971]\textsuperscript{\cite{SmithKrajewski}})} $($resp. 
\uwave{$\lambda$-discretely almost expandable}
\index{$\lambda$-discretely almost expandable| \S\ref{sec:ch3_3}\hfill}$)$ 
if every locally finite  $($resp. discrete$)$ $\lambda$-family 
of $($closed$)$ subsets of $X$ has a locally finite open expansion.



**almost $\mathfrak{m}$-expandable** (Smith and Krajewski 1972) (Resp. **almost $\mathfrak{m}$-discretely expandable**) if every LF (resp. discrete) $\mathfrak{m}$-family of (closed) subsets of $X$ has a point-finite open expansion.

\textup{(iii)}  $X$ is **$\mathfrak{m}$-subexpandable** (Smith 1975) (Resp. **$\mathfrak{m}$-discretely subexpandable**) if every LF (resp. discrete) $\mathfrak{m}$-family of (closed) subsets of $X$ has a $\sigma$-LF sequence of open expansions.

\textup{(iv)} $X$ is **$\mathfrak{m}$-$\theta$-expandable** (Katuta 1975) (Resp. **$\mathfrak{m}$-discretely $\theta$-expandable**) if every LF (resp. discrete) $\mathfrak{m}$-family of (closed) subsets of $X$ has a $\theta$-sequence of open expansions.

\textup{(v)}  $X$ is **discretely subexpandable** (Katuta 1975) if every discrete family of closed subsets of $X$ has a $\theta$-sequence of open expansions.
}

{\theorem{}  For any $X$, the following are equivalent:

\textup{(i)} $X$ is countably paracompact.

\textup{(ii)} Every countable increasing open cover of $X$ has a $\sigma$-cushioned open refinement.

\textup{(iii)} Every countable increasing open cover of $X$ has an open shrink.

\textup{(iv)}$X$ is countably expandable.
}
