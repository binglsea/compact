	\section{Expandable spaces} \label{sec:ch3_3}


**3.3.1. Definition.**

(i) $X$ is **$\mathfrak{m}$-expandable** (Krajewski 1972) (Resp. **$\mathfrak{m}$-discretely expandable**) if every LF (resp. discrete) $\mathfrak{m}$-family of (closed) subsets of $X$ has a LF open expansion.

(ii) $X$ is **almost $\mathfrak{m}$-expandable** (Smith and Krajewski 1972) (Resp. **almost $\mathfrak{m}$-discretely expandable**) if every LF (resp. discrete) $\mathfrak{m}$-family of (closed) subsets of $X$ has a point-finite open expansion.

(iii) $X$ is **$\mathfrak{m}$-subexpandable** (Smith 1975) (Resp. **$\mathfrak{m}$-discretely subexpandable**) if every LF (resp. discrete) $\mathfrak{m}$-family of (closed) subsets of $X$ has a $\sigma$-LF sequence of open expansions.

(iv) $X$ is **$\mathfrak{m}$-$\theta$-expandable** (Katuta 1975) (Resp. **$\mathfrak{m}$-discretely $\theta$-expandable**) if every LF (resp. discrete) $\mathfrak{m}$-family of (closed) subsets of $X$ has a $\theta$-sequence of open expansions.

(v) $X$ is **discretely subexpandable** (Katuta 1975) if every discrete family of closed subsets of $X$ has a $\theta$-sequence of open expansions.

---

**3.3.2. Theorem.** For any $X$, the following are equivalent:

i). $X$ is countably paracompact.

ii). Every countable increasing open cover of $X$ has a $\sigma$-cushioned open refinement.

iii). Every countable increasing open cover of $X$ has an open shrinking.

iv). $X$ is countably expandable.

---

*(Note: "LF" stands for **locally finite** in this context.)*
