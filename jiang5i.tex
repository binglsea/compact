\section{Normal covers and $D\omega^2$-refinable spaces}
\label{sec:normal_covers}
{\theorem{} For any open cover $\mathcal{U}$ of $X$, the following are equivalent.
	
\textup{(i)}  $\mathcal{U}$ is a normal cover.

\textup{(ii)} $\mathcal{U}$ has a $\sigma$-disjoint functionally open refinement $\mathcal{V} = \bigcup_{n<\omega} \mathcal{V}_n$ such that each $\bigcup \mathcal{V}_n$ is functionally open.

\textup{(iii)}  $\mathcal{U}$ has a $\sigma$-star finite functionally open refinement 
$\mathcal{V} = \bigcup_{n<\omega} \mathcal{V}_n$ such that each $\bigcup \mathcal{V}_n$ is functionally open.
}

Proof. 
(i) $\Rightarrow$ (ii). Suppose $\mathcal{U}$ is normal. By Theorem 2.1.6, $\mathcal{U}$ has a functionally open refinement $\mathcal{V} = \bigcup_{n<\omega} \mathcal{V}_n$ where each $\mathcal{V}_n$ is discrete, so is disjoint, and by Lemma 1.3.8, $\bigcup \mathcal{V}_n$ is functionally open.

$(2) \Rightarrow (3)$. Trivial.

$(3) \Rightarrow (1)$. Suppose $\mathcal{U}$ is an open cover of $X$ and $\mathcal{V} = \bigcup_{n<\omega} \mathcal{V}_n$ is a functionally open refinement of $\mathcal{U}$ such that each $\mathcal{V}_n$ is star-finite and $\bigcup \mathcal{V}_n$ is functionally open. By Theorem 2.1.3, $\mathcal{V} = \bigcup_{n<\omega} \mathcal{V}_n$ has a star-finite functionally open refinement $\mathcal{W}$. Since $X$ is UP, $\mathcal{W}$ is a normal cover of $X$.
