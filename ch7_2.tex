\section{Paracompact and Expandable Spaces} \label{sec:7.2}



7.2.1 Definition. Let $X$ be a space and $\mathcal{U}, \mathcal{V}$ be covers of $X$.

 (i) $\mathcal{V}$ is a local $\omega$-refinement of $\mathcal{U}$ at $x \in X$ if there is $A \in \mathcal{T}(x)$ and $V \in \mathcal{V}$ such that...


 (ii) $\mathcal{V}$ is a local star-refinement of $\mathcal{U}$ at $x$ if there is $F \in \mathcal{C}(x)$ such that...



$\mathcal{V}$ is a local $\omega$-refinement (local star-refinement) of $\mathcal{U}$ iff $\mathcal{V}$ is a local $\omega$-refinement (local star-refinement) of $\mathcal{U}$ at each $x \in X$.

A sequence of covers $\langle\mathcal{V}_n\rangle$ is a local $\omega$-refining sequence (local star-refining sequence) for $\mathcal{U}$ if for each $x \in X$ there is $n < \omega$ such that $\mathcal{V}_n$ is a local $\omega$-refinement (local star-refinement) of $\mathcal{U}$ at $x$.

7.2.2. Lemma. Let $\mathcal{U}, \mathcal{V}$ be covers of $X$.

 (i) If $\mathcal{V}$ is a local $\omega$-refinement of $\mathcal{U}$ at $x$, then $\mathcal{V}$ is a local star-refinement of $\mathcal{U}$ at $x$.


 (ii) If $\mathcal{V}$ is a local star-refinement of $\mathcal{U}$ at $x$, then $\mathcal{V}$ is a point-star refinement of $\mathcal{U}$ at $x$.
