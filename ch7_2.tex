\section{Paracompact and Expandable Spaces} \label{sec:7.2}



{\definition{\label{def:7.2.1}} 
Let $X$ be a space and $\mathscr{U}, \mathscr{V}$ be covers of $X$.

 (i) $\mathscr{V}$ is a local $\omega$-refinement of $\mathscr{U}$ at $x \in X$ if there is $A \in \mathscr{T}(x)$ and $V \in \mathscr{V}$ such that...


 (ii) $\mathscr{V}$ is a local star-refinement of $\mathscr{U}$ at $x$ if there is $F \in \mathscr{C}(x)$ such that...



$\mathscr{V}$ is a local $\omega$-refinement (local star-refinement) of $\mathscr{U}$ iff $\mathscr{V}$ is a local $\omega$-refinement (local star-refinement) of $\mathscr{U}$ at each $x \in X$.

A sequence of covers $\langle\mathscr{V}_n\rangle$ is a local $\omega$-refining sequence (local star-refining sequence) for $\mathscr{U}$ if for each $x \in X$ there is $n < \omega$ such that $\mathscr{V}_n$ is a local $\omega$-refinement (local star-refinement) of $\mathscr{U}$ at $x$.
}


{\lemma{} \label{lem:7.2.2}Let $\mathscr{U}, \mathscr{V}$ be covers of $X$.

 (i) If $\mathscr{V}$ is a local $\omega$-refinement of $\mathscr{U}$ at $x$, then $\mathscr{V}$ is a local star-refinement of $\mathscr{U}$ at $x$.


 (ii) If $\mathscr{V}$ is a local star-refinement of $\mathscr{U}$ at $x$, then $\mathscr{V}$ is a point-star refinement of $\mathscr{U}$ at $x$.
}
