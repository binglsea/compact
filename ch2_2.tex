 {\theorem{} \textup{(Michael [1953, 1957]\textsuperscript{\cite{Michael1953}\cite{Michael1957}})}

\textup{(i)} $X$ is $T_2$ paracompact.

\textup{(ii)}  Every open cover of $X$ has a $\sigma$-discrete open refinement.

\textup{(iii)} Every open cover has a $\sigma$-locally finite open refinement.

\textup{(iv)}  Every open cover has a $\sigma$-closure-preserving open refinement.

{\theorem{} \textup{(Morita [1962a]\textsuperscript{\cite{Morita1962}})} 
	$X$ is $\lambda$-fully normal iff $X$ is normal and $\lambda$-paracompact.
}

\begin{proof}
($\Rightarrow$) By \ref{coro:2_1_8}.
	
$(\Leftarrow)$ Suppose that $X$ is normal and $\lambda$-paracompact and 
that $\mathcal{U} = \{U_\alpha : \alpha < \lambda\}$ be an open cover of $X$.
Then $X$ has a locally finite open cover $\mathcal{V} = \{V_\alpha : \alpha < \lambda\}$ 
such that $V_\alpha \subset U_\alpha$ for each $\alpha < \lambda$. 
By Engelking [1977]\textsuperscript{\cite{Engelking}} and Theorem 1.5.18, % TO_DO
there is an open cover $\mathcal{W} = \{W_\alpha : \alpha \in A\}$ of $X$ such that $\overline{W}_\alpha \subset V_\alpha$ for each $\alpha \in A$. For each $\alpha \in A$, there is a functionally open set $H_\alpha$ such that $\overline{W}_\alpha \subset H_\alpha \subset V_\alpha$. Then $\mathcal{H} = \{H_\alpha : \alpha \in A\}$ is a LF functionally open refinement of $\mathcal{U}$. By Theorem 2.1.6, $X$ is $\aleph$-fully normal, so $X$ is $\aleph$-fully normal.
\end{proof}
