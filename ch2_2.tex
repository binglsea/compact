 2.2. Paracompactness with separation axioms

**2.2.1. Theorem.** (Michael (1953, 1957)).
For any $T_1$ regular space $X$, the following are equivalent:

1. $X$ is $\kappa$-paracompact.


2. Every open cover of $X$ has a $\sigma$-discrete open refinement.


3. Every open cover has a LF open refinement.


4. Every open cover has a $\sigma$-cp open refinement.



*Proof.* See Corollary 5.2.9.

**2.2.2. Theorem.** (Michael (1959)). For any $T_1$ space $X$, the following are equivalent:

1. $X$ is $\kappa$-paracompact.


2. Every open cover has a $\sigma$-cushioned open refinement.


3. Every open cover has a cushioned refinement.
