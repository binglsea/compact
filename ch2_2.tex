\textup{(i)} $X$ is $T_2$ paracompact.

\textup{(ii)}  Every open cover of $X$ has a $\sigma$-discrete open refinement.

\textup{(iii)} Every open cover has a $\sigma$-locally finite open refinement.

\textup{(iv)}  Every open cover has a $\sigma$-closure-preserving open refinement.

{\theorem{} \textup{(Morita [1962]\textsuperscript{\cite{Morita1962}})} 
	$X$ is $\lambda$-fully normal iff $X$ is normal and $\lambda$-paracompact.
}

\begin{proof}
($\Rightarrow$) By Corollary \ref{co:2_1_8}.
	
$(\Leftarrow)$ Suppose that $X$ is normal and $\lambda$-paracompact, and 
that $\mathscr{U} = \{U_\alpha : \alpha < \lambda\}$ be an open cover of $X$.
Then $X$ has a locally finite open cover $\mathscr{V} = \{V_\alpha : \alpha < \lambda\}$ 
such that $V_\alpha \subset U_\alpha$ for each $\alpha < \lambda$. 
By Theorem 1.5.18 of Engelking [1977]\textsuperscript{\cite{Engelking}}, 
there exists an open cover $\mathscr{G} = \{G_\alpha : \alpha \in \lambda\}$ of $X$ 
such that $\overline{G}_\alpha \subset V_\alpha$ for each $\alpha \in \lambda$. 
For each $\alpha \in \lambda$, there is a functionally open set $H_\alpha$ such that $\overline{G}_\alpha \subset H_\alpha \subset \overline{H_\alpha}\subset V_\alpha$. 
Then $\mathscr{H} = \{H_\alpha : \alpha \in \lambda\}$ is a localy finite
 functionally open refinement of $\mathscr{U}$. 
 By Theorem \ref{th:2_1_6}, 
 $\mathscr{U}$ is  normal, so $X$ is $\lambda$-fully normal.
\end{proof}
