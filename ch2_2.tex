{\theorem{} \textup{(Morita [1962a]\textsuperscript{\cite{Morita1962}})} 
	$X$ is $\lambda$-fully normal iff $X$ is normal and $\lambda$-paracompact.
}

\begin{proof}
$(\Leftarrow)$ Suppose that $X$ is normal and $\aleph$-paracompact and that $\mathcal{U} = \{U_\alpha : \alpha \in A\}$ be an open cover of $X$ ($\vert{}A\vert{} \le \aleph$). Then $\mathcal{U}$ has a LF open cover $\mathcal{V} = \{V_\alpha : \alpha \in A\}$ such that $V_\alpha \subset U_\alpha$ for each $\alpha \in A$. By Engelking [1977], Theorem 1.5.18, there is an open cover $\mathcal{W} = \{W_\alpha : \alpha \in A\}$ of $X$ such that $\overline{W}_\alpha \subset V_\alpha$ for each $\alpha \in A$. For each $\alpha \in A$, there is a functionally open set $H_\alpha$ such that $\overline{W}_\alpha \subset H_\alpha \subset V_\alpha$. Then $\mathcal{H} = \{H_\alpha : \alpha \in A\}$ is a LF functionally open refinement of $\mathcal{U}$. By Theorem 2.1.6, $X$ is $\aleph$-fully normal, so $X$ is $\aleph$-fully normal.
\end{proof}
