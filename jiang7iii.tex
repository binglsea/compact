\section{Metacompact and Almost Expandable Spaces} \label{sec:7.1}

{\lemma{} \label{lem:7.1.1}
If $X$ is $\lambda$-almost expandable $($or $\lambda$-expandable$)$, then 
every $A\omega^2$-$\lambda$-cover of $X$ has a point-finite $($or locally finite$)$
open refinement.
}

\begin{proof} 
Suppose $X$ is $\lambda$-almost expandable (or $\lambda$-expandable) and 
let $\mathscr{U} = \{U_\alpha : \alpha < \lambda\}$ be an 
$A\omega^2$-$\lambda$-cover of $X$. 
Let $\mathscr{A} = \bigcup_{m, n < \omega}\mathscr{A}_{m,n}$ be a refinement 
of  $\mathscr{U}$ such that each $\mathscr{A}_{m,n} = \{A_{m,n,\alpha} : \alpha < \lambda\}$ 
is a locally finite family of subsets of 
$X\setminus \bigcup\{\bigcup\mathscr{A}_{p,q}: (p,q) < (m,n)\}$, 
with  $A_{m,n,\alpha} \subset U_\alpha$ for each $\alpha < \lambda$. 
We establish the following

\textsc{Claim}. For each $m, n \in \omega$, there exists a point-finite (or locally finite) family 
$\mathscr{U}_{m,n} = \{U_{m,n,\alpha} : \alpha \in \lambda\}$ 
of open sets in $X$ such that $U_{m,n,\alpha} \subset V_\alpha$ 
for each $\alpha$ and

$$\bigcup_{\langle p,q \rangle \le \langle m,n \rangle} \left(\bigcup \mathscr{A}_{p,q}\right) \subset \bigcup_{\langle p,q \rangle \le \langle m,n \rangle} \left(\bigcup \mathscr{V}_{p,q}\right)$$

\textsc{Proof of Claim}. We prove this by induction on 
$(\omega \times \omega, <)$. 
Suppose $\mathscr{A}_{0,0} = \{\emptyset\}$ and let $\mathscr{V}_{0,0} = \{\emptyset\}$.
Let $m, n \in \omega$ and assume that the point-finite (or locally finite) 
open families $\mathscr{V}_{p,q} = \{V_{p,q,\alpha} : \alpha \in \lambda\}$ 
have been constructed such that the Conditions above are satisfied 
for all $\langle p,q \rangle < \langle m,n \rangle$. 
Let $L = \bigcup \{ \bigcup \mathscr{V}_{p,q} : \langle p,q \rangle < \langle m,n \rangle \}$. 
By the inductive hypothesis we have 
$$\bigcup_{\langle p,q \rangle < \langle m,n \rangle} \left(\bigcup \mathscr{A}_{p,q}\right) \subset L.$$ 
The family $\mathscr{A}_{m,n}|_{(X\setminus L)}$ is 
locally finite in $X$, so it has a point-finite (or locally finite) 
pen expansion $\mathscr{V}_{m,n} = \{V_{m,n,\alpha} : \alpha < \lambda\}$ 
such that for each $\alpha < \lambda$, 
$A_{m,n}\setminus L \subset V_{m,n}\subset U_\alpha$. 
Then $\bigcup\mathscr{A}_{m,n}\setminus L \subset \bigcup \mathscr{V}_{m,n}$. 
It follows from inductive hypothesis that 
$$\bigcup_{(p,q)\le(m,n)}\left(\bigcup \mathscr{A}_{p,q}\right) \subset L\cup\left(\bigcup(\mathscr{A}_{m,n}\right) \subset 
\bigcup_{(p,q)\le(m,n)}\left(\bigcup \mathscr{V}_{p,q}\right).$$
Thus $\mathscr{V}_{m,n}$ is the desired family and the \textsc{Claim} is proved.

By Theorem \ref{th:4.1.10} (\ref{th:4.1.9}),  $X$  is countably metacompact 
(or countably paracompact). There exists a point-finite (or locally finite) open cover 
$\mathscr{W} = \{W_{m,n}: m,n < \omega\}$ of  $X$  such that 
$W_{m,n} \subset \bigcup\mathscr{V}_{m,n}$ for each $m, n < \omega$. 
For $\alpha < \lambda$, let $G_\alpha = \bigcup\{W_{m,n}\cap V_{m,n,\alpha}: m,n<\omega\}$. 
Then $\mathscr{G} = \{G_\alpha : \alpha < \lambda\}$ is an open refinement 
of $\mathscr{U}$. We are to show:

(1) $\mathscr{G}$ is point-finite (or locally finite).

\noindent We only prove $\mathscr{G}$ is  locally finite. Let $x \in X$. There exists 
 $H_0\in \operatorname{top}(x)$
such that $\operatorname{ind}(\mathscr{W})_{H_0}$ is finite. For each $m, n < \omega$, 
there exists $H_{m,n}\in  \operatorname{top}(x)$ such that $\operatorname{ind}(\mathscr{V}_{m,n})_{H_{m,n}}$ is finite. 
Let $H = H_0 \cap(\bigcap\{H_{m,n}:(m,n)\in \operatorname{ind}(\mathscr{W})_{H_0}\})$. 
Then $H\in\operatorname{top}(x)$ and
$$\operatorname{ind}(\mathscr{G})_H \subset 
\bigcup\{\operatorname{ind}(\mathscr{V}_{m,n})_{H_{m,n}}: (m,n)
\in\operatorname{ind}(\mathscr{W})_{H_0}\}.$$ 
Hence $\operatorname{ind}(\mathscr{G})_H$ is finite and $\mathscr{G}$ is locally finite.
\end{proof}

{\lemma{} \label{lem:7.1.2}
If  $X$  is $\lambda$-discretely almost expandable, every $D\omega$-cover of  $X$  has a point-finite open refinement.
}

\begin{proof}
Suppose that  $X$  is $\lambda$-discretely almost expandable and that 
$\mathscr{U} = \{U_\alpha : \alpha < \lambda\}$ is a $D\omega$-cover of  $X$ . 
Let $\mathscr{F} = \bigcup_{n<\omega} \mathscr{F}_n$ be a refinement of $\mathscr{U}$ 
such that each $\mathscr{F}_n = \{F_{n,\alpha} : \alpha \in \lambda\}$ 
is a discrete closed family of subsets of  $X\setminus\bigcup_{i<n}(\bigcup \mathscr{F}_i)$
with $F_{n,\alpha}\subset U_\alpha$ for each $\alpha < \lambda$, 
and $\bigcup_{i<n}(\bigcup \mathscr{F}_i)$ is closed in  $X$  
for each $n >0$.

\textsc{Claim}. For each $n < \omega$  there exists a point-finite family $\mathscr{V}_n = \{V_{n,\alpha} : \alpha < \lambda\}$ of open sets in $X$ such that for each $n < \omega$,

(i)\textsubscript{$n$} $V_{n,\alpha} \subset U_\alpha$ for each $\alpha < \lambda$,

(ii)\textsubscript{$n$}   $\bigcup_{i \le n} (\bigcup \mathscr{F}_i) \subset \bigcup_{i \le n} (\bigcup \mathscr{V}_i)$ and

(iii)\textsubscript{$n$}    $(\bigcup_{i < n} (\bigcup \mathscr{F}_i)) \bigcap
(\bigcup \mathscr{V}_n) = \emptyset$. 

\noindent\textsc{Proof of Claim}. We use induction on $n < \omega$. Assume 
$\mathscr{F}_0 = \{ \emptyset \}$ and let $\mathscr{V}_0 = \{ \emptyset \}$. 
Let $n \ge 1$ and suppose that for each $i \le n$ the point-finite open family 
$\mathscr{V}_i = \{V_{i,\alpha} : \alpha < \lambda\}$ in $X$ has been constructed and Conditions
(i)\textsubscript{$i$} -- (iii)\textsubscript{$i$} are satisfied.

Then $(i)_{n+1} \rightarrow \dots$
$\bigcup_{i \le n+1} (\bigcup \mathscr{V}_i) \subset \bigcup_{i \le n+1} (\bigcup \mathscr{V}_i) \dots$ Let $L = \bigcup_{i \le n} (\bigcup \mathscr{V}_i)$.
For each $\alpha < \lambda$, $F_{n+1,\alpha} - L \subset U_\alpha - \bigcup_{i \le n} (\bigcup \mathscr{V}_i)$. Since $\mathscr{U}_{n+1}/(X-L)$ is a point-finite discrete closed family in $X$,  there exists  is a point-finite open family $\mathscr{V}_{n+1} = \{V_{n+1,\alpha} : \alpha < \lambda\}$ in $X$ such that
$F_{n+1,\alpha} - L \subset V_{n+1,\alpha} \subset U_\alpha - \bigcup_{i \le n} (\bigcup \mathscr{V}_i)$
for each $\alpha < \lambda$. Then $\bigcup F_{n+1,\alpha} - L \subset \bigcup \mathscr{V}_{n+1} \subset X - \bigcup_{i \le n} (\bigcup \mathscr{V}_i)$.
It follows that $\dots$ for each $\dots$ and
$(\bigcup_{i \le n+1} (\bigcup \mathscr{V}_i)) - (\bigcup_{i \le n} (\bigcup \mathscr{V}_i)) = \dots$, so the conditions $(i)_{n+1}$ and $(iii)_{n+1}$ are satisfied. To see $(ii)_{n+1}$, we show
$\bigcup_{i \le n+1} (\bigcup \mathscr{V}_i) \subset \dots \subset \bigcup_{i \le n+1} (\bigcup \mathscr{V}_i)$.
This completes the induction and the proof of claim.

By the Claim,  there exists a sequence $\dots$ of point-finite families of open sets in $X$ such that $\mathscr{V} = \bigcup_{n<\omega} \mathscr{V}_n$ is an open refinement of $\mathscr{U}$. For each $x \in X$,  there exists $m < \omega$ such that $x \in \bigcup \mathscr{V}_m$.
For each $k \ge m$, $\dots$
$\mathscr{W}_x = \bigcap_{n=m}^{\infty} \operatorname{ind}(\mathscr{V}_n)_{x_0}$,
so by $(ii)$ $\dots$ Thus $\operatorname{ind}$ is point-finite.
\end{proof}


7.1.3. Theorem. For any $X$, the following are equivalent:

(i) $X$ is $\sigma$-almost expandable ($\sigma$-discretely almost expandable).


(ii) Every $A\omega$ ($D\omega$)-$\sigma$-cover of $X$ has a point-finite open refinement.


(iii) Every $\mathscr{A}\omega$ ($\mathscr{D}\omega$)-$\sigma$-cover of $X$ has an IP open pointwise $\omega$-refinement.


(iv) Every directed $A\omega$ ($D\omega$)-$\sigma$-cover of $X$ has an IP open point-star refinement.


(v) Every IP directed $A\omega$ ($D\omega$)-$\sigma$-cover of $X$ has a semi-open point-star refinement.


(vi) Every IP directed $A\omega$ ($D\omega$)-$\sigma$-cover of $X$ has a CP closed refinement.


(vii) Every IP directed $A\omega$ ($D\omega$)-$\sigma$-cover of $X$ has a cushioned refinement.



Proof.

(i) $\Rightarrow$ (ii): By Lemma 7.1.1 (7.1.2).


(ii) $\Rightarrow$ (iii) and (vi) $\Rightarrow$ (vii): Clear.


(iv) $\Rightarrow$ (vi): By Lemma 4.1.6.


(v) $\Rightarrow$ (vii): By Theorem 4.1.9.


(vii) $\Rightarrow$ (i): Assume (vii) and let $\mathscr{B}(\mathscr{F})$ be 
a LF (discrete closed) $\sigma$-family in $X$. By $\dots$, the induced cover $\mathscr{B}(\mathscr{F})$ has a cushioned refinement. By Lemma 1.2.3 (1.2.4), $\mathscr{B}(\mathscr{F})$ has a point-finite open expansion, so (i) holds.

7.1.4. Corollary. Metacompact spaces are $\sigma$-almost expandable.

7.1.5. Theorem. For any $X$, the following are equivalent:

(i) $X$ is $\sigma$-metacompact.


(ii) (Jiang 1987) $X$ is $\sigma$-almost expandable and $\sigma$-$A\omega$-refinable.


(iii) $X$ is $\sigma$-discretely almost expandable and $\sigma$-strictly quasi-paracompact.



Proof. (i) $\Rightarrow$ (ii) and (iii): By Corollary 7.1.4 and Theorem 7.1.3.

7.1.6. Corollary. For any $X$, the following are equivalent:

(i) $X$ is metacompact.


(ii) (..., 1984), Long (1985) $X$ is discretely almost expandable and strictly quasi-paracompact.


(iii) (Burke 1973) $X$ is discretely almost expandable and submetacompact.



Proof. By Theorem 7.1.5.
