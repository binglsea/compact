\section{Metacompact and Almost Expandable Spaces} \label{sec:7-1}


7.1.1. Lemma. If $X$ is $\lambda$-almost expandable ($\lambda$-expandable), then every $A(\lambda)$-open cover of $X$ has a point-finite (locally finite) open refinement.

Proof. Suppose $X$ is $\lambda$-almost expandable ($\lambda$-expandable) and let $\mathscr{V} = \{V_\alpha : \alpha < \lambda\}$ be an 
$A(\lambda)$-open cover of $X$. Let $\mathscr{A}_{m,n}$ be a refinement of 
$\mathscr{V}$ such that each $\mathscr{A}_{m,n} = \{A_{m,n,\alpha} : \alpha \in \lambda\}$ 
is a locally finite family of subsets of $X$, with 
$A_{m,n,\alpha} \subset V_\alpha$ for each $\alpha \in \lambda$. We establish the following

Claim. For each $m, n \in \omega$, there is a point-finite (locally finite) family 
$\mathscr{U}_{m,n} = \{U_{m,n,\alpha} : \alpha \in \lambda\}$ 
of open sets in $X$ such that $U_{m,n,\alpha} \subset V_\alpha$ 
for each $\alpha$ and

$$\bigcup_{\langle p,q \rangle \le \langle m,n \rangle} \left(\bigcup \mathscr{U}_{p,q}\right) \subset \bigcup_{\langle p,q \rangle \le \langle m,n \rangle} \left(\bigcup \mathscr{A}_{p,q}\right)$$

$\langle m,n \rangle \in \omega \times \omega$.

and let $\mathscr{U}_{0,0} = \{\emptyset\}$.

open families $\mathscr{U}_{p,q} = \{U_{p,q,\alpha} : \alpha \in \lambda\}$

for all $\langle p,q \rangle < \langle m,n \rangle$. Let $L = \bigcup \{ \bigcup \mathscr{U}_{p,q} : \langle p,q \rangle < \langle m,n \rangle \}$.
