\section{Collectionwise Normal Spaces} \label{sec:8-1}
	

8.1.1. Definition ([Bing 1951]\textsuperscript{\cite{Bing}}). $X$ is $\kappa$-collectionwise normal if every discrete $\kappa$-family of closed subsets of $X$ has a discrete (or equivalently, disjoint) open expansion.

8.1.2. Definition. $X$ is $\kappa$-discretely expandable if every discrete $\kappa$-family of closed subsets of $X$ has a $\sigma$-sequence of open expansions.

8.1.3. Theorem (Jiang [1992]). $X$ is $\kappa$-collectionwise normal iff $X$ is $\kappa$-discretely expandable.

Proof. $\Rightarrow$ clear.

For each $n \in \omega$, $\mathcal{V}_n\vert{}_{H_n}$ is a point-finite open family in the subspace $H_n$.
