\chapter*{Preface}

\indent\indent
Paracompact spaces were introduced by 
Dieudonné [1944]\cite{Dieudonne}
 as a natural generalization 
of compact topological spaces. On the other hand, fully normal spaces were 
defined by Tukey [1940]\cite{Tukey}
 as a generalization of metric spaces. It is perhaps 
surprising that these two generalizations coincide, since 
Stone [1948] \cite{Stone}
proved that a Hausdorff space is paracompact iff it is fully normal. 
In fact, the paracompact spaces provide one of the most successful common 
generalizations of compact spaces and metric spaces, and thus constitute 
one of the most important classes of spaces in point-set topology.

Paracompact spaces and the five related classes of covering properties 
are fundamental. Their characterization theorems are rich and colorful. 
The study of characterization theory for these basic covering properties 
has continued for more than 80 years since the publication of Tukey's paper [1940].

This book presents a systematic account of this theory. In Chapters 2 -- 4, 
we present results published between 1940 and 1985, while Chapters 5 -- 8 
present results obtained by the author. Some of the characterization 
theorems appeared in Jiang [1987] -- [1992]\cite{Jiang1987a}
 or in the manuscript Jiang [2015]. 
But the newest results appear here for the first time.
