\section{Subparacompact and Discretely Subexpandable spaces} \label{sec:6.2}


6.2.1. Theorem. For any space $X$, the following are equivalent:

(i) $X$ is a-discretely subexpandable.

(ii) Every $D\omega$ $\omega$-cover of $X$ has a $\sigma$-discrete closed refinement.

(iii) Every $D\omega$ $\omega$-cover of $X$ has a $\sigma$-sequence of point-star refining open refinements.

(iv) Every $D\omega$ $\omega$-cover of $X$ has a $\sigma$-sequence of point-star refining open covers.

(v) Every IP $D\omega$ $\omega$-cover of $X$ has a sequence of IP open covers of $X$.

(vi) Every IP $D\omega$ $\omega$-cover of $X$ has a sequence of semi-open covers of $X$.

(vii) Every IP $D\omega$ $\omega$-cover of $X$ has a cushioned refinement.

Proof. (i) $\implies$ (ii). Assume (i) and let $\mathcal{U}$ be a $D\omega$ $\omega$-cover of $X$. Then $X$ is discretely subexpandable. By Theorem 6.1.2 and 3.1.5, there is a countable closed cover $\{F_n\}$ of $X$ such that each $\mathcal{U} \cap F_n$ has a point-finite open refinement $\mathcal{V}_n$ in the subspace $F_n$. Since the closed subspace $F_n$ of $X$ is $\sigma$-discretely subexpandable by Theorem 3.3.7, $\mathcal{V}$ has a discrete closed refinement $\mathcal{F}_n$ in $F_n$. Then $\bigcup \mathcal{F}_n$ is a $\sigma$-discrete closed refinement of $\mathcal{U}$.
