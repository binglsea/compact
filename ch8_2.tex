\section{Fully Normal Spaces} \label{sec:8.2}

8.2. Fully Normal Spaces

8.2.1. Theorem

For any space $X$, the following are equivalent:

(i) $X$ is fully normal.

(ii) $X$ is shrinkable and $\aleph_0$-screenable.

(iii) $X$ is countably shrinkable and every open $\kappa$-cover of $X$ has a $\sigma$-star-finite open refinement.

Proof. $(\text{i}) \Rightarrow (\text{ii})$ By Corollary 2.1.8.

$(\text{ii}) \Rightarrow (\text{iii})$ Clear.
