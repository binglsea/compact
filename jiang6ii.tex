\section{Submetacompact and $\lambda$-expandable Spaces} \label{sec:6-1}

In order to characterize the $\lambda$-expandable spaces, [Katuta 1975] introduced the $\lambda$-$A$-cover. An open cover $\mathscr{U}$ of a space $X$ is an $A_\lambda$-cover if it has a LF refinement. We now generalize this notion as follows.

{\definition{\label{def:6.1.6}} 
(i) An open cover $\mathscr{U} = \{U_\alpha\}_{\alpha \in \Lambda}$ of $X$ 
is an $A_\lambda^\omega$-cover if it has a refinement 
$\mathscr{B} = \bigcup_{n<\omega} \mathscr{B}_n$ 
where each $\mathscr{B}_n = \{B_{n,\alpha}\}_{\alpha \in \Lambda}$ 
is a LF family of subsets of the subspace 
$X \setminus \bigcup_{i<n} (\bigcup \mathscr{B}_i)$ with 
$B_{n,\alpha} \subset U_\alpha$ for each $\alpha \in \Lambda$ 
and $\bigcup \mathscr{B}_n$ is closed in 
$X \setminus \bigcup_{i<n} (\bigcup \mathscr{B}_i)$ for each $n < \omega$.

(ii) An open cover $\mathscr{U}$ is an $A_{\lambda}^{\text{c}\omega}$-cover 
if $\mathscr{U}$ has a refinement 
$\mathscr{A} = \bigcup_{m,n<\omega} \mathscr{A}_{m,n}$ 
where each $\mathscr{A}_{m,n}$ is a LF family of subsets of the 
subspace $X \setminus \dots$ where $\le$ be a well ordering on 
$\Lambda$ as in Definition 6.1.3.

(iii) A space $X$ is $\lambda$-$A_\lambda^\omega$-refinable ($\lambda$-$A_{\lambda}^{\text{c}\omega}$-refinable) if every open 
$\lambda$-cover of the space is an $A_\lambda^\omega$-cover ($A_{\lambda}^{\text{c}\omega}$-cover).
}
