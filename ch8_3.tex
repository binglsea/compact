\section{Strongly Paracompact and Ultraparacompact Spaces} \label{sec:8.3}


{\theorem{\label{th:8.3.1}} 
For any \(\lambda\) the following are equivalent:

\textup{(i)} \(X\) is \(\lambda\)-strongly paracompact.

\textup{(ii)} Every open \(\lambda\)-cover of \(X\) has a star-finite open refinement.

\textup{(iii)}  Every open \(\lambda\)-cover of \(X\) has a star-finite closed refinement.

\textup{(iv)} Every open \(\lambda\)-cover of \(X\) has a star-finite refinement.
}

\begin{proof}
(i) \(\Rightarrow\) (ii) \(\Rightarrow\) (iv) Clear.

(ii) \(\Rightarrow\) (iii) Let \(\mathscr{U}\) be an open \(\lambda\)-cover of \(X\). There is a star-finite open refinement \(\mathscr{V}\) of \(\mathscr{U}\). Since \(X\) is regular, there is an open refinement \(\mathscr{W}\) of \(\mathscr{V}\) such that \(\{\overline{W}\mid W\in\mathscr{W}\}\) refines \(\mathscr{V}\). Since \(\mathscr{W}\) is star-finite, \(\{\overline{W}\mid W\in\mathscr{W}\}\) is a star-finite closed refinement of \(\mathscr{U}\).

(iii) \(\Rightarrow\) (i) Let \(\mathscr{U}\) be an open \(\lambda\)-cover of \(X\). There is a star-finite closed refinement \(\mathscr{F}\) of \(\mathscr{U}\). For each \(F\in\mathscr{F}\), choose \(U_F\in\mathscr{U}\) with \(F\subset U_F\). For each \(F\in\mathscr{F}\), let \(V_F=X\setminus\bigcup\{F'\in\mathscr{F}\mid F'\cap F=\emptyset\}\). Then \(V_F\) is an open set containing \(F\). Let \(W_F=V_F\cap U_F\). Then \(\mathscr{W}=\{W_F\mid F\in\mathscr{F}\}\) is a star-finite open refinement of \(\mathscr{U}\).

(iv) \(\Rightarrow\) (iii) Let \(\mathscr{U}\) be an open \(\lambda\)-cover of \(X\). There is a star-finite refinement \(\mathscr{A}\) of \(\mathscr{U}\). For each \(x\in X\), let \(N_x\) be an open neighborhood of \(x\) intersecting only finitely many members of \(\mathscr{A}\). Then \(\mathscr{N}=\{N_x\mid x\in X\}\) has a star-finite refinement \(\mathscr{B}\). Then \(\{\overline{B}\mid B\in\mathscr{B}\}\) is a star-finite closed refinement of \(\mathscr{U}\). 

\end{proof}

(i) \(X\) is \(\lambda\)-ultraparacompact.

(ii) \(X\) is countably paracompact and every open \(\lambda\)-cover of \(X\) has a disjoint open refinement.

(iii) Every open \(\lambda\)-cover of \(X\) has a disjoint closed refinement.
}

Proof. (i) \(\Rightarrow\) (ii) and (i) \(\Rightarrow\) (iii) Clear.
(ii) \(\Rightarrow\) (i) Let \(\mathscr{U}\) be an open \(\lambda\)-cover of \(X\). There is a disjoint open refinement \(\mathscr{V}\) of \(\mathscr{U}\). Since \(\mathscr{V}\) is disjoint, \(\mathscr{V}\) is a partition of \(X\) by open sets, so members of \(\mathscr{V}\) are also closed. Thus, \(\mathscr{V}\) is a disjoint open refinement.

(iii) \(\Rightarrow\) (i) Let \(\mathscr{U}\) be an open \(\lambda\)-cover of \(X\). There is a disjoint closed refinement \(\mathscr{F}\) of \(\mathscr{U}\). For each \(F\in\mathscr{F}\), choose \(U_F\in\mathscr{U}\) with \(F\subset U_F\). Since \(\mathscr{F}\) is a disjoint closed cover of \(X\), each \(F\in\mathscr{F}\) is also open. Thus, \(\mathscr{F}\) is a disjoint open refinement of \(\mathscr{U}\). \(\square\)

(i) \(X\) is \(\lambda\)-ultraparacompact.
(ii) Every open \(\lambda\)-cover of \(X\) has a star-countable open refinement.
(iii) Every open \(\lambda\)-cover of \(X\) has a star-countable closed refinement.

Proof. (i) \(\Rightarrow\) (ii) Clear.
(ii) \(\Rightarrow\) (iii) Let \(\mathscr{U}\) be an open \(\lambda\)-cover of \(X\). There is a star-countable open refinement \(\mathscr{V}\) of \(\mathscr{U}\). Since \(X\) is ultraparacompact, \(X\) is zero-dimensional. For each \(V\in\mathscr{V}\), \(V\) can be partitioned into clopen sets. Thus, there is a star-countable closed refinement of \(\mathscr{U}\).

(iii) \(\Rightarrow\) (i) Similar to the proof of 8.3.1. \(\square\)
